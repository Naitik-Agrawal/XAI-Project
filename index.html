📑 Architectural Comparison: Base Paper vs. XAI-ZT-P2P

IEEE / ACM Research Manuscript Breakdown
Dimension Base Paper (Urmila & Shubham, 2025) Our New Research Discovery (XAI-ZT-P2P)
Trust Paradigm Static Perimeter Trust: Single RSA-2048 handshake at startup; peer permanently trusted thereafter. Continuous Zero-Trust ("Never Trust, Always Verify"): Continuous behavioral monitoring over a 14-dimensional runtime telemetry vector.
Threat Detection None: Zero anomaly detection; insider nodes can poison chunks, flood requests, or snoop without detection. Proactive ML Risk Engine: Random Forest ensemble tuned with 5-fold cross-validation on 12,000 peer sessions (94.33% Accuracy, 93.96% Precision).
Explainability (XAI) None: AI is limited strictly to local YOLOv3 optical character recognition and image labeling. TreeSHAP Game-Theoretic Explainability: Computes exact local feature attribution vectors (φi), providing auditable explanations for throttled/blocked peers.
Access Control Binary (All-or-Nothing): Peer is either connected or disconnected. 4-Tier Stratified Policy Matrix:
• ALLOW (<0.30): 64 KB, 50 MB/s
• RESTRICT (0.30–0.60): 32 KB, 5 MB/s
• VERIFY_QUARANTINE (0.60–0.80): 16 KB, Nonce Challenge
• BLOCK (≥0.80): Socket terminated, ban.
Protocol Adaptation Static 64 KB chunks (32 KB fallback solely upon generic TCP buffer congestion). Threat-Adaptive Protocol Orchestration: Dynamic socket throttling, chunk buffer resizing, and ephemeral cryptographic challenges tied to real-time risk scores.
Inference Overhead Uninstrumented for security evaluation. 18.79 ms mean scoring latency, ensuring zero impact on the base paper's sub-50ms UDP discovery goal.
